Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Wholeday → Cancel subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.

I've changed phone – how do I get Premium back?
Sign in with the same Wholeday account and tap Me → Settings → Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

I can't sign in from the home-screen app.
Tap "Email me a sign-in code" and type the code from the email straight into the app.

How do I share my home, a trip, my cycle or join a trainer?
Each one has a step-by-step guide in the app – look for "How it works" next to the code, or Me → Settings → Help & guides.

How do I delete my account?
Me → Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes everything. If you subscribe through Apple, cancel the subscription too.

Is my data safe?
Yes – it's encrypted, locked to your account and never sold. See our privacy policy for the details.
