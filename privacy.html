3. Our lawful bases

  • Contract – to provide the app and any subscription you've chosen.
  • Explicit consent – for health, wellbeing and cycle data (special category data). You give this by choosing to enter it, and can withdraw it at any time by deleting it or turning the feature off.
  • Consent – for optional AI features and for connecting Apple Health. You'll be asked first and can withdraw it in Settings.
  • Legitimate interests – to keep the service secure, prevent abuse (such as bots and AI over-use) and fix problems.
  • Legal obligation – where the law requires us to keep or share information (for example tax records).

4. How we keep your data safe

  • Encrypted – everything is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored encrypted at rest by our database provider.
  • Locked to your account – database rules (row-level security) mean each account can only ever read its own data and what's been shared with it. These rules are enforced by the database itself, not just the app.
  • Passwords are stored hashed, need at least 8 characters, and sign-ins are protected against bots. You can sign in with a one-time code instead of a password.
  • Strict app security policy – the app only runs its own code and blocks anything injected, and links to unsafe addresses are stripped out.
  • Secret keys stay on our servers – keys for AI and other services are never inside the app.
  • Our own accounts are protected with two-step login, and access is limited to the owner.
  • Backups are taken daily by our database provider. Your journal can also be locked with a PIN.
  • If something goes wrong – if there's a data breach that's likely to put you at risk, we'll tell you, and report it to the ICO within 72 hours where the law requires.

No system can be 100% secure, but we take every reasonable step and keep improving.

5. Who can see your data

Only you, unless you share it – and sharing is always your choice:

  • Shared home: items you mark as shared are visible to members of your home. Your journal is never shared.
  • Trainer: if you join a trainer, they can see your workouts, food diary, weigh-ins and steps – nothing else. You can leave any time.
  • Partner cycle: if you share your cycle code, people who join see your period start dates and the phase and tips worked out from them – not your symptoms or notes.
  • Trip groups: everyone in the group can see and edit that trip, including who has paid what.

7. International transfers

Some providers (for example Anthropic, RevenueCat and Resend) process data in the United States. Where data leaves the UK we rely on safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account yourself at any time in Me → Settings → Delete my account, which permanently deletes your data straight away (backups roll off within 30 days). AI usage counts are kept for up to 12 months to manage limits and costs. Purchase records are kept as long as tax law requires. If you don't use your account for 2 years we may email you and then delete it.

9. Your rights

You can access your data, correct it, delete it, download it (Settings → Backup), object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email privacy@wholeday.co.uk – we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

10. Children

Wholeday is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 16.

11. Changes

If we make important changes we'll tell you in the app and ask you to agree again.
